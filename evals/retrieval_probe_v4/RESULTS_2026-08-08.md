## Error anatomy and boundary conditions

The pass is useful but deliberately narrow:

1. The verified-procedure lane recovered `procedure-atlas-worker-deploy`, but ranked the also
   verified integration-test procedure first on the deploy query. Set recall succeeded; an
   automatic action would still have selected the wrong procedure.
2. Every one of the eight no-result/wrong-scope queries produced a non-empty final bundle. Dense
   Top-5 was responsible for candidates on all eight. Candidate discovery therefore has no
   defensible automatic-application or orientation-injection semantics.
3. Exact tags contributed a relevant candidate to only 1/12 quality queries and produced one
   abstention false candidate. Normalizing an opaque cue such as `ORBIT_TELEMETRY_CANARY` can expose
   the generic in-scope `telemetry` tag, which is evidence of related vocabulary, not applicability.
4. Exact lifecycle following recalled both correction queries with perfect abstention, but the
   Redis query followed two differently typed supersession chains and also surfaced the current
   handoff. Lifecycle edges need typed intent or ownership before they can imply relevance.
5. The v3 failures and query texts were known before v4 was designed. This is a diagnostic
   ablation, not evidence that the hand-built cues generalize.

## Decision

- Preserve the v3 decision: no dense-only retrieval, deterministic auto-apply, semantic candidate
  injection, or fused cross-lane score.
- The promising product slice is an **explicit candidate search** response with separate lane
  labels, durable scope, lifecycle status, verification state, evidence, and a strict result cap.
  The caller must still inspect evidence and decide applicability.
- Do not add the candidate bundle to the lean orientation packet. Orientation should remain small,
  high-confidence, and governed by its separately passing probe.
- Do not treat exact tags as entity aliases. Add graph behavior only after Engram has an
  authoritative, testable entity-to-memory ownership edge and typed relationships.
- The next retrieval experiment should use a newly frozen adversarial corpus and unseen queries,
  then gate both recall and candidate burden/precision. It should test typed lifecycle edges,
  procedure task discrimination, tag confounders, and no-result abstention. A reranker or
  evidence-entailment gate should be evaluated only from an attested local model or through the
  separately approved provider pilot.

No production retrieval, storage, orientation, adapter, or harness behavior changed in v4.
