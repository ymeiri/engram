## Failure anatomy

1. **Procedure discrimination:** the integration-test procedure ranked first for a deploy-command
   query; the relevant deploy procedure ranked second. Kind and receipt verification establish
   eligibility, not task applicability.
2. **Typed lifecycle intent:** `Redis with Streams` correctly found the current queue decision but
   also followed the unrelated handoff supersession chain. An untyped `supersedes` traversal is too
   broad to imply query relevance.
3. **Rule cue confounders:** generic `policy`, `when`, and `may` wording surfaced the deploy-window
   rule for customer retention and erasure questions.
4. **Tag confounders:** `telemetry` inside an unrelated opaque cue and a satellite query surfaced
   Atlas observability. Exact normalized vocabulary is not an entity or applicability link.
5. **Handoff confounder:** a Snowflake resume query surfaced the Atlas queue-worker handoff.
6. **Dense burden:** dense Top-5 ensured every no-result query received plausible-looking noise,
   including cross-domain Android, GPU, Snowflake, and satellite questions.

## Decision

- Reject the v4 deduplicated bundle as a default explicit-search product surface. The earlier v4
  result remains valid only as evidence that multiple lanes can maximize recall on known failures.
- Preserve the stronger prior decisions: no bundle in lean orientation, no score fusion, no
  semantic auto-injection, no inferred applicability, and no automatic procedure execution.
- Do not tune v4 thresholds or cues on this holdout. Preserve v5 as falsification evidence.
- Narrowly invoked and typed operations remain plausible: exact stable-key lookup, explicitly
  filtered kind search, typed lifecycle inspection, and condition-aware verified procedure match.
  They must not be silently combined into one response.
- The next discriminating experiment should evaluate Engram's existing verified-procedure matcher
  directly. Freeze cases for correct task and conditions, same-kind wrong procedure, missing and
  mismatched prerequisites, expired proof, missing receipt, superseded versions, moved worktrees,
  and cross-project canaries. Require perfect forbidden-result and abstention behavior before
  considering a harness-facing procedure slice.

No production behavior changed in v5. The live provider-backed Codex/Claude comparison remains
separately approval-gated.
