## What the failures isolate

The production arm missed all three repository-scoped procedure/decision queries and the stable
repository-record key. The same records were recovered by both baselines once the stable remote
was supplied to their scope filter. The immediate defect is therefore repository-identity
propagation into memory search, not evidence that BM25 is required.

All three lexical arms missed `message broker selection` → the active NATS JetStream decision and
`Redis Streams` → its superseding active decision. Those misses require a semantic and/or
lifecycle-link candidate-generation arm; lexical weighting alone cannot recover a record with no
shared terms.

All three arms produced plausible Atlas matches for a query containing the unmatched
Orbit-specific cue `ORBIT_ONLY_CANARY`. Scope filtering prevented leakage, but none of the arms had
an absolute relevance or unknown-cue rule strong enough to abstain.

## Decision boundary

Do not choose BM25, dense, hybrid, graph, or agentic retrieval from this run. First propagate the
resolved repository identity through the production search contract and rerun this unchanged
protocol. Then add model-pinned dense, hybrid, and supersession-link expansion arms to a new frozen
protocol. A production choice needs the same zero-leakage and abstention gates plus a meaningful
recall/MRR improvement over the corrected lexical control.
