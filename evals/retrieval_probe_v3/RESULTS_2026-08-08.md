## Error anatomy

The three unsafe automatic applications were:

1. `what verified command releases the Atlas queue consumer?` → worker observability. Dense
   similarity was high (0.5893), but the relevant verified deploy procedure did not appear in the
   dense Top-5 or current lexical Top-5.
2. `change GraphQL persisted-query CDN policy` → Atlas HTTP client. A misleading 0.0878 margin
   crossed the frozen margin rule.
3. `debug GPU kernel occupancy regression` → `debug with bits`. Lexical/dense agreement and the
   word `debug` were shallow evidence, not applicability.

Dense candidate discovery also missed the deploy-window rule and verified deploy procedure. The
current lexical arm found the deploy-window rule, while neither arm found the procedure. Across
the 12 quality queries, the union of current and dense candidates covered 11; the remaining miss
requires kind-aware/procedure-aware candidate generation or a stronger semantic model.

The exact opaque-cue rule again rejected both cross-project canaries, and exact all-query-term
lifecycle matching safely followed the Redis and isahc supersession edges. Those narrow rules
remain defensible. Broad similarity, margin, and lexical/dense agreement do not.

## Decision

- Do not ship dense-only memory retrieval.
- Do not ship the frozen deterministic auto-apply selector.
- Do not automatically inject semantic memory candidates into orientation or context packets.
- Keep the repository-identity scope correction already implemented; it passed both retrieval
  probes with zero scope leakage.
- If adding a product slice before a stronger applicability model is available, expose separate,
  explicitly labeled lexical and semantic **search candidates** only. Do not fuse their scores,
  call them applicable, or feed them into automatic context.
- The next retrieval experiment should add structured candidate lanes, especially procedure kind,
  verification state, aliases/entities, and lifecycle edges, then measure set recall. A reranker or
  evidence-entailment gate should be evaluated only from an attested local model or through the
  separately approved provider pilot.

The provider-backed host-native pilot remains approval-gated and was not run.
